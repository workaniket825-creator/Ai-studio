// The Seven Gates

Sequential decisions, each with an owner

The framework deliberately separates business decisions from SME knowledge contributions from data scientist technical decisions. Every gate has named ownership. Every dimension has a user-controllable verdict (Pass / Fail / Pending) with a rationale capture. Every dimension has free-text notes for context that structured questions cannot capture.

00
Gate · Entry Criteria · MANDATORY

Pre-Framework Qualification

Three quick qualification checks that must pass before entering the framework. Business qualified (clear problem, use case category, KPIs), Technical qualified (baseline model tested, gap documented), Data qualified (data exists, SME available). If any of three fails — STOP. Do not proceed.

Business SME Data Scientist
01
Gate · Business & Problem Framing

Use Case Specification

What business process is impacted? Is the model assistive, advisory, or autonomous? What happens if it's wrong? Does it require deterministic output, explainability, auditability? This gate is owned by Business with SME validation — the foundation of every downstream decision.

Business · Lead SME · Validate
02
Gate · Model Gap & Feasibility

Why Fine-Tuning?

Tested baseline models. Documented gaps. Ruled out alternatives (prompting, RAG, agentic orchestration, public models). Classified the gap (knowledge / reasoning / consistency / formatting). Assessed domain niche-ness and hallucination risk — the strongest signals for whether DAPT is essential or SFT will suffice. Most projects fail because this isn't done.

Data Scientist · Lead SME · Joint
03
Gate · Data Readiness

Data Contract for Fine-Tuning

Volume, quality, coverage, format, modality, bias, compliance, consent. Plus Historic Intelligence & Competitive Data — does the organisation hold proprietary historic decisions and expert judgements that no competitor has? This is often the strongest argument for fine-tuning: the data is the moat.

Data Scientist · Lead SME · Validate Business · Sponsor
04
Gate · Model Design & Tuning Strategy

Execution Blueprint

Base model category. Tuning approach. Modality, language, context window, task characteristics. Latency SLA, cost-per-query target, deployment environment. Plus an explicit Domain Adaptation Decision — DAPT/CPT first, or directly to SFT/LoRA? Reasoned from niche-ness and historic intelligence.

Data Scientist · Lead
05
Gate · Risk, Governance & Control

Controlled Deployment

Risk level classified. Human-in-the-loop requirements. Pass/fail thresholds at output. Rejection conditions and escalation rules. Fallback strategy — base model or rule-based. The deliverable: Risk matrix and control mechanisms ready for governance sign-off.

Business · Lead Risk & Compliance
06
Gate · Evaluation & Acceptance Criteria

Success Definition

Metrics (accuracy, F1, hallucination rate, consistency, latency). Test strategy (benchmark dataset, blind testing, edge cases). SME validation tasks. Acceptance criteria. Go-live conditions. Without these defined before training, the program survives on faith rather than evidence.

Data Scientist SME Business
07
Gate · Consulting Recommendations

Tech Platform & Recommendations

The consultant's free-text recommendation gate. Capture tech platforms (Mistral Forge, NVIDIA NeMo, AWS Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry, etc.), specific recommended models, reasons for choices, primary observations, concerns, opportunities, and final consulting recommendation. Intentionally open-ended — this is the section the LLM relies on most when generating the final report.

// What's new in v3

Built for customer collaboration

User Verdict Control

Pass / Fail / Pending — you decide

Every dimension carries a user-controlled verdict toggle. The framework helps you reason; the user calls the verdict. Each verdict captures a rationale — preserving the "why" alongside the "what".

Free-Text Capture

Notes everywhere it matters

Every dimension has a "Detailed Response" textarea for context structured questions can't capture. Every verdict has a rationale field. All captured in JSON export — the assessment becomes a complete document, not just a checklist.
